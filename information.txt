csv file:
coloumns having in the dataset
1. age (in yrs)
2. sex - gender
3. cp(chest pain type) (4 values)
types of chest pain
    value 0 : typical angina
    value 1: atypical angina
    value 2: non-anginal pain
    value 3: asymptomatic
4. trestbps: resting blood pressure (in mm Hg on admission to the hospital)
5. chol (cholesterol): serum cholestoral in mg/dl
6. fbs: - blood sugar level after fasting (fasting blood sugar > 120 mg/dl) (1= ture; 0= false)
7. restecg: - {ECG results} resting electro cardiographic results
    value 0: normal
    value 1: having ST-T wave abnormally (T wave inversions and/r ST elevation or depression of > 0.05mV)
    value 2: showing probable or definite left ventricular hypertrophy by Estes' riteria
8. thalach: maximum heart rate achieved
9. exang: - (chest pain during exercise)exercise induced angina (1=yes; 0=no)
10. oldpeak = ST depression induced by exercise relative to rest
11. slope: the slope of the peak exercise ST segment
    valye 1: upsloping
    value2: flat
    value3: downsloping
12. ca: number of major vessels (0-3) colored by flourscopy
13. thal(thalassemia): 3= normal; 6= fixed defect; 7 = reversable defect
14. target(output variable): 0=less chance of heart attact, 1= more chance of heart attack
